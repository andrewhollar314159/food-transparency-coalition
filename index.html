A LEGAL BLIND SPOT

Companies aren’t required to tell you what’s behind “natural flavors” — not on the label, not even if you ask.

REAL HEALTH CONSEQUENCES

33 million Americans have food allergies.1 Every 10 seconds, a food allergy reaction sends someone to the ER.1

1. FARE (Food Allergy Research & Education), National Indicator Report on Food Allergy, 2026.

OUR SOLUTION

One FDA rule: every ingredient and processing aid, named in a free public database anyone can check.
