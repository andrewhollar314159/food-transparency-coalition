THE FOOD TRANSPARENCY COALITION

About Us

We are a coalition of nonprofits and consumer advocates fighting for your right to know what is in your food.

Our Vision

We envision a future US food system that is fully transparent. No hidden ingredients. No unlisted incidental additives. A food system where anyone can easily access every single ingredient and additive used in foods without having to jump through hoops. Consumers deserve to know all the details about what they are putting in their bodies, both for safety and personal choice.

Our Mission

To advance policy solutions that increase transparency in the US food system and improve safety and freedom of choice for consumers.
