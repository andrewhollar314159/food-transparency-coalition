Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

We don’t ask for your name or email, and we don’t use cookies, analytics, ads, or tracking. We don’t sell or share personal information. If you sign the petition, you do that on the U.S. government’s Regulations.gov website, not with us.

Who we are

This policy covers foodtransparency.org, the website of the Food Transparency Coalition (“we,” “us,” or “our”). It explains what information is involved when you visit this site and what happens to it.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through this site. There are no accounts, sign-up forms, contact forms, or mailing lists here, and we don’t ask you to enter anything.

Information our service providers receive

Hosting. This site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel’s servers automatically receive basic technical details when your browser requests a page, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request. Vercel uses this to deliver the site and keep it secure, as described in Vercel’s own privacy policy. We do not use this information to identify or track visitors.

Fonts. This site’s typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. To deliver them, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and basic browser details. Google’s handling of that information is covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising, social media pixels, or any other tracking tools.

The share button

The “Share the link” button uses your device’s built-in share menu or copies the site address to your clipboard. This happens on your device. Nothing is sent to us, and we don’t see who you share with.

Signing the petition

The petition links take you to Regulations.gov, the U.S. government’s public rulemaking website. Anything you submit there, including your name, comment, or other details, goes to the FDA, not to us. It is governed by Regulations.gov’s own privacy and terms, and comments are generally made public. Please read the instructions there before submitting anything you want to keep private. We do not receive or control those submissions.

Stories and #EveryIngredient

If you post about your experience using #EveryIngredient, your post is shared on the social media platform you choose and is governed by that platform’s privacy policy and settings. Public posts can be seen by anyone, so think about what personal or health details you include. We will only feature a personal story on this site with the permission of the person who shared it.

If we add videos or other content hosted by another service, that service may collect information or set cookies when you play it. We will update this policy before that happens.

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, including coalition members’ sites and Regulations.gov. Once you leave this site, the other site’s privacy policy applies, not this one.

Children’s privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If what this site collects changes, for example if we add a sign-up form or analytics, we will update this policy first. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
