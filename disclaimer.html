Who we are

Foodtransparency.org is the website of the Food Transparency Coalition (“we,” “us,” or “our”), a group of organizations and individuals asking the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to require full disclosure of every ingredient and processing aid in packaged food. The information on this site (“this site”) is shared in good faith to explain that effort and invite public support.

We are not physicians, medical professionals, scientists, or lawyers, and nothing on this site is professional advice of any kind. We make no representation or warranty, express or implied, that anything on this site is accurate, complete, current, or reliable. It may contain errors.

Not medical or dietary advice

This site describes ingredients such as carmine, glycerin, and maltodextrin and notes where they can come from, along with possible concerns for people with alpha-gal syndrome, food allergies, or religious or ethical dietary needs. These are general examples meant to show why clearer labels matter. They are not a complete or authoritative list, and the source of any ingredient in a specific product varies by manufacturer and can change at any time.

Do not use anything on this site to decide whether a particular food is safe for you or anyone else, or to make decisions about diagnosis, treatment, diet, or nutrition. For those decisions, consult a physician or other qualified professional.

The example label and the proposed database

“Sunny Farms Strawberry Lemonade” and its ingredient lists are invented for illustration. They do not describe any real product or company. The free public ingredient database described on this site does not exist yet. It is what our petition asks the FDA to create, and its final form, if any, will be decided by the FDA.
